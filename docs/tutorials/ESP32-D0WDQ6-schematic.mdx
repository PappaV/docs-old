# ESP32‑D0WDQ6 Schematic Example

This page provides a **schematic‑only** example of an ESP32‑D0WDQ6 module circuit, mirroring the approach used in the Hardware Academy tutorial.

The schematic is displayed below. All components are from the JLCPCB library, making the design ready for ordering.

![ESP32‑D0WDQ6 Schematic](/img/ESP32-D0WDQ6/schematic.png)

## How to use this schematic

1. **Copy the design** – Click the "Copy" button in the top‑right corner of the schematic view on the tscircuit editor to duplicate the circuit into your own project.
2. **Adjust footprints** – If you need alternative footprints, replace the parts with equivalents from the JLCPCB library.
3. **Generate Gerbers** – Once satisfied, generate the Gerber files and upload them to JLCPCB for fabrication.

Feel free to modify the circuit to suit your specific requirements, such as adding power regulation, external antennas, or additional peripherals.
