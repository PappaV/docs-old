## Overview

This tutorial provides a **schematic‑only** example of an ESP32‑D0WDQ6 module circuit. It mirrors the approach used in the Hardware Academy tutorial, focusing on the wiring and component connections without a PCB layout.

## Schematic

<Image src="/img/ESP32-D0WDQ6/schematic.png" alt="ESP32‑D0WDQ6 schematic" />

The schematic shows the essential connections:
- Power supply (3.3 V) to the ESP32 VCC pins.
- Decoupling capacitors close to the power pins.
- A reset circuit with a pull‑up resistor and a push‑button.
- Basic UART pins for serial communication.
- Optional external crystal and load capacitors.

Feel free to copy this schematic into your own projects and adapt the component values as needed.
