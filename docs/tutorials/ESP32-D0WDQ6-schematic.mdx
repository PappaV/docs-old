## Overview

This tutorial provides a **schematic‑only** reference for wiring an ESP32‑D0WDQ6 module on a PCB. It mirrors the example from the Hardware Academy, focusing solely on the schematic diagram.

## Schematic

<Image src="/img/ESP32-D0WDQ6/schematic.png" alt="ESP32‑D0WDQ6 schematic" />

The schematic shows the essential connections:

- **Power**: 3.3 V supply to the ESP32 VCC pin.
- **Ground**: Common ground for the module and peripheral components.
- **UART**: TX0/RX0 pins for serial communication.
- **GPIOs**: Example pins for LED, button, and I²C.
- **Decoupling Capacitors**: 0.1 µF caps close to the VCC pins.

Feel free to adapt the schematic to your specific design requirements.
